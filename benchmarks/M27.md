is not met. The M24 offered load gives group commit nothing to group.

## Finding

All 1,200 acknowledged write batches still published one log object each.
The harness offers writes one at a time at 250 ms intervals and each executes
in well under that, so two writes are never queued together and a group never
forms. Group commit only coalesces concurrently queued writes; it adds no
acknowledgement delay. The PUT reduction came from fewer pack and root
publications, not from logs. Meeting 4 PUT/s at this arrival pattern would
require holding acknowledgements across arrivals (over 250 ms each, which
breaks the write p95 gate) or client-side batching. Write execution and
cold-query p95 rose against M24 but stay within their gates; this run does not
isolate the cause.
